MegaBrain Studios

An independent studio that produces original video content for its own channels.

What we do

We write, produce and publish original short-form and long-form video across our own channels on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Everything we publish is content we created ourselves.

MegaBrain Publisher

MegaBrain Publisher is our internal publishing tool. It runs on our own computer and its only job is to take a video we have already produced and upload it to our own accounts on the platforms listed above.

It is not a product offered to the public. It has no sign-up, no customers and no users other than us. On TikTok it uploads to our own account's inbox as a draft, which a person on our team then reviews and posts from the TikTok app.
