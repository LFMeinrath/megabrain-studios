Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

Summary

MegaBrain Publisher has no users other than us. It is an internal tool that uploads our own videos to our own accounts. It collects no data from the public, has no sign-up, and shows no advertising.

1. This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, set cookies or embed trackers here. GitHub may keep standard server logs for the requests it serves; see GitHub's own privacy statement for how it handles those.

2. What the tool accesses

When we authorize MegaBrain Publisher against one of our own social media accounts, the platform issues an access credential for that account.

On TikTok the tool requests only:

  • video.upload — to place a video we produced into our own account's inbox as a draft;
  • user.info.basic — to confirm which of our accounts the authorization belongs to, so a video is never sent to the wrong channel.

On Facebook and Instagram (Meta Graph API), for our own Facebook Page and the Instagram account linked to it, the tool requests only:

  • pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement — to identify which of our own Pages the authorization belongs to, so a video is never sent to the wrong one;
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish a video we produced to our own Page;
  • instagram_basic — to identify the Instagram account linked to that Page;
  • instagram_content_publish — to publish a video we produced to that Instagram account.

The tool does not read other people's profiles, followers, messages or videos, does not read comments or direct messages, does not run advertising, and does not request any permission beyond those listed above.

3. Data we store, and where

The authorization credentials are written to a file on the operator's own computer, readable only by that operating-system user, and are never transmitted anywhere except back to the platform that issued them. We do not run a server that holds them, and we do not store them in any code repository.

Alongside those credentials we keep our own production records: which of our videos went to which of our channels, and when.

4. Data about other people

We do not collect, buy, store, process or sell personal data belonging to members of the public. No third party has an account with us, because there are no accounts.

5. Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. The only network requests the tool makes are to the official APIs of the platforms we publish to, using our own credentials.

6. Retention and revocation

Credentials are kept only while the tool is in use, and are deleted when it is retired. We can revoke the tool's access to any of our accounts at any time from the platform's own settings, which immediately invalidates the credential.

7. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not accessible to the public at all.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the tool changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

9. Contact

Enquiries about this policy can be directed to the developer account registered for MegaBrain Publisher on the platform concerned — TikTok for Developers, or Meta for Developers (app “publicação automatica”).

10. Deleting our data

Because the tool holds no data belonging to anyone but us, there is no third-party data to delete on request. We remove our own authorization at any time from the platform’s own settings — on Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, and on TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions — which immediately invalidates the credential, and we then delete the credential file from the operator’s computer.
